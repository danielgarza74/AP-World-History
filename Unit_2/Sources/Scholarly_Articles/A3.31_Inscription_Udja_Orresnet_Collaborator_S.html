Selection Overview

Lloyd reads the statue inscription of Udjahorresnet, an Egyptian official who served Cambyses and Darius I, as a collaborator's apology, arguing that he accepted Persian rule because the Great King "was prepared to satisfy his personal and local interests" and because Egyptian thought let him recast Cambyses as a true Pharaoh and champion of cosmic order. Lloyd stresses that the text's stereotyped language makes historical details uncertain, and contrasts this acceptance with the open hostility of Egyptians toward later Persian kings who "showed a total disregard for Egyptian susceptibilities."
