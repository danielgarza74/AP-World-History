Selection Overview

Warder places original Buddhism among the rival Śramaṇa schools of eastern India (Ājīvikas, Jainas, Lokāyatas), which opposed Brahmanical ritual and hereditary religious authority. He ties these schools to the rise of autocratic states whose kings patronized them, and he argues that after the Mauryas the kings made a compromise with a reviving Brahmanism.
