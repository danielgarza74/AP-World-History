Selection Overview

Pines tests Sima Qian's account of the state of Qin against excavated inscriptions and finds the Shiji wrong about Qin's "barbarian" marginality and its break with the Zhou house. Rather than a deliberate Han-era agenda, he traces these biases to Qin's own court records, which may have been altered to justify fourth-century reforms, combined with "the widespread eastern prejudices against Qin."
