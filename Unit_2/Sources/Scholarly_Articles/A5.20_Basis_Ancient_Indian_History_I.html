Selection Overview

Kosambi argues that the spread of agrarian village settlement over tribal country was "the first great social revolution in India," and that caste formed as tribes and guilds were enrolled "in a hierarchical scheme of general society, under brahmin sanction." Reading land-grant inscriptions in Marxist terms, he treats caste as class at a low level of production, fluid while settlement expanded and hardening only as villages ingrew; this is Part I of a two-part article.

We must note the difference between this later brahminism and the far earlier type which had developed within the tribe.
